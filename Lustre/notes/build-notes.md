# Build notes: Lustre video
- Same pipeline as the GPFS video: custom skia-python 2D renderer at 1920x1080 logical scaled 2x to 3840x2160, raw BGRA piped to ffmpeg libx264 (veryfast, crf 18, tune animation), 30 fps; Kokoro-ONNX am_michael at speed 1.04, one synthesis per narration line, line start times (timing.json cues) drive the animations; mux pads each scene's audio to its frame count, concatenates, loudnorm -16 LUFS, AAC 192k.
- Research order: Wikipedia history + release table, wiki.lustre.org admin architecture page, OLCF Orion page, insideHPC Orion article, CFS 2002 white paper, Schwan 2003 Linux Symposium paper, LUG 2023 Orion slides. Facts, conflicts and hedges are in lustre-first-principles-notes.md.
- Fixes during build: Orion first-pass line said "first number one" (not supported; changed to "was number one in 2022"); ASR check found mispronunciations (Braam, Coda, InterMezzo, inode, RoCE, ldiskfs, ext4, byte-range) and I respelled them in script.py ("Bram", "Koda", "Inter Mezzo", "eye-node", "Rocky", "L disk F S", "ext four", "bite-range") and re-synthesized s1,s2,s5,s6,s7,s9 ("by-trained" still shows up in ASR for "bite-range"; I could not tell whether a human ear hears it, so please listen to s5 and s9); fx.py server/node/disk ignored alpha for fills (ghost shapes before fade-in), fixed; the striping-balance picture first showed an unrepresentative trial (6.8x) while narration said "almost four times", so the scene now picks a trial close to the 300-trial mean (3.6x vs 3.80x mean) and labels it "one typical trial".
