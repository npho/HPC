# Build notes: BeeGFS video
- Same pipeline as the GPFS and Lustre videos: custom skia-python 2D renderer at 1920x1080 logical scaled 2x to 3840x2160, raw BGRA piped to ffmpeg libx264 (veryfast, crf 18, tune animation), 30 fps; Kokoro-ONNX am_michael at speed 1.04, one synthesis per narration line; line start times (timing.json cues) drive the animations; mux pads each scene's audio to its frame count, concatenates, loudnorm -16 LUFS, AAC 192k.
- Research: Wikipedia, doc.beegfs.io (architecture, striping, mirroring, system requirements, tuning pages, RDMA, caching, BeeOND, 8.0 release notes), beegfs.io edition table, HPCwire 2018, Next Platform 2018, NetApp blog 2019. Facts, conflicts, and hedges: beegfs-first-principles-notes.md.
- Gaps stated in the video rather than papered over: no founding paper found; no locking/consistency semantics found in the docs; chunk-size default differs between two doc pages; vendor claims (CSIRO, ABCI) flagged.
- Fixes during build: (1) the shared drawing helper in lx.py labelled storage disks "OSTs" (a Lustre term); I caught it on the final spot-check and changed it to "targets", then re-rendered only s0 and s3 and re-muxed; (2) one caption in s3 sat close to a server label and was moved up; (3) the sources panel in s10 was timed to appear after the narration reached it and its staggered reveal was shortened so all lines show before the video ends.
- Speech check (faster-whisper base.en): "BeeGFS" is transcribed as "BGFS" and Lustre as "Luster" by the recognizer; I treat those as recognizer artifacts, not necessarily mispronunciations, but I have not listened end to end.
- Not done: no human end-to-end viewing by me; the stripe-balance chart, peer-count example and RDMA memory estimate are my own and labelled on screen.
